Debt Review Process - NCA

  1. Free Assessment: We check if you qualify as over-indebted.
  2. Form 17.1: We notify all credit providers you are under review.
  3. Proposal: We negotiate reduced payments and interest.
  4. Court Order: Legal protection from legal action and repossession.
  5. Single Payment: You pay one reduced amount to a Payment Distribution Agency.
  6. Clearance: Once paid, you receive a clearance certificate.
Important: You cannot take new credit while under debt review.
