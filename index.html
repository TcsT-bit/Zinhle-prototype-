Debt Review Process

  1. Assessment: We check if you are over-indebted under the NCA.
  2. Application: We send Form 17.1 to all credit providers.
  3. Proposal: We negotiate lower payments with creditors.
  4. Court Order: If accepted, you get a court order protecting you.
  5. Payment: You pay one reduced amount monthly to a PDA.
  6. Clearance: Once paid, you get a clearance certificate.

Note: You cannot take new credit while under debt review.
